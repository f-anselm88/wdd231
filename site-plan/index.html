1. Site Name

Eraka Lyetu

The name means “our language” in Rukwangali. It tells visitors right away that the site belongs to the Rukwangali-speaking community and exists to keep the language alive and in daily use.

2. Site Purpose

  • Teach Rukwangali vocabulary, phrases, and pronunciation through short daily lessons.
  • Give learners a way to practice with spaced-repetition flashcards so words are remembered long term.
  • Share a bilingual reading section (Rukwangali and English) so learners can read real text.
  • Motivate regular practice with progress tracking, streaks, and points.
  • Support language preservation by digitizing Rukwangali in a form young people want to use.

3. Scenarios

These questions represent what the target audience will ask when they arrive.

  1. A young Namibian who grew up in Windhoek understands some Rukwangali but cannot speak it well: “Where can I practice speaking with correct pronunciation for only ten minutes a day?”
  2. A missionary, teacher, or aid worker moving to the Kavango region: “What are the most useful words and greetings I should learn first?”
  3. A parent in the diaspora: “How can I help my children learn our language when we do not live near family?”
  4. A returning learner: “How do I see which words I am forgetting and review them?”

4. Color Schema

The palette is inspired by the green riverbanks and warm earth tones of the Kavango region. This document uses the same colors.

  • River Green – #1F4D3A
    Header, footer, main headings, and navigation background.
  • Kavango Ochre – #C8872B
    Buttons, links on hover, and highlights such as streak and points badges.
  • Sand Cream – #FAF6EE
    Page background and card backgrounds, keeping reading areas calm.
  • Charcoal – #1E1E1E
    Body text for strong contrast on the cream background.

5. Typography

  • Merriweather (bold) is used for the site name and all headings. Its sturdy serif letters feel trustworthy and cultural.
  • Source Sans 3 (regular and semi-bold) is used for body text, buttons, and navigation. It is clean and easy to read on small phone screens, and it handles Rukwangali characters well.

Both fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and are applied to this document, with Georgia and Arial as fallbacks.
