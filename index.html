About Me

I'm a junior full-stack developer based in Windhoek, Namibia, originally from the Kavango region and a native Rukwangali speaker. I'm pursuing a BS in Software Development through BYU-Idaho and BYU-Pathway Worldwide, working across React, JavaScript, C#, Python, and REST APIs. Before development, I spent two years serving a mission in Ghana, work that shaped how I approach problems now — patiently, cross-culturally, and with an eye for what actually helps people. I also run The Catalist, a small advertising and retail business, which keeps me fluent in shipping real products for real clients.
