Community & culture

Life along the river

The Kavango region sits along the Kavango River in northeastern Namibia, bordering Angola. Many Vakwangali communities live close to the river, where fishing, farming, and fetching water are part of daily life — which is why so many everyday Rukwangali phrases reference water, baskets, and the land.

Oral tradition

Proverbs carry a lot of weight in Rukwangali-speaking communities — a short saying can settle an argument or gently correct a child faster than a long lecture. A few are included in the phrasebook, under the "proverbs" filter.
