Join the Chamber

Complete the application below to become a member of the Windhoek Chamber of Commerce. Choose the membership level that fits your organization — every level connects you with the Khomas business community.

Membership Levels

  • NP Membership

    For non-profit organizations. No membership fee.

  • Bronze Membership

    An affordable entry point for new and small businesses.

  • Silver Membership

    Added visibility and discounts for growing businesses.

  • Gold Membership

    Maximum exposure and benefits for established businesses.

NP Membership

Free membership for registered non-profit organizations.

  • Listing in the member directory
  • Invitations to chamber networking events
  • Access to community volunteer opportunities

Bronze Membership

An affordable starting point for small and new businesses.

  • Everything in NP Membership
  • Discounted rates on chamber training workshops
  • Business listing with contact details in the directory

Silver Membership

Added visibility and savings for growing businesses.

  • Everything in Bronze Membership
  • Featured placement in the online directory
  • Discounted admission to the annual trade expo
  • Eligible for member spotlight on the homepage

Gold Membership

Maximum exposure and benefits for established businesses.

  • Everything in Silver Membership
  • Priority homepage spotlight placement
  • Complimentary event sponsorship opportunity each quarter
  • Free admission for two to all chamber events
